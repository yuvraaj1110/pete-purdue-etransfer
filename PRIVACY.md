# Privacy Policy — Pete (Purdue e-Transfer)

_Last updated: 2026-07-13_

Pete is an unofficial browser extension that checks whether a college course transfers to
Purdue University West Lafayette, using Purdue's own public Transfer Credit Course Equivalency
Guide.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Pete has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no server of its own.

- Course lookups go **directly from your browser to Purdue's public website**
  (`selfservice.mypurdue.purdue.edu`) — the same site you could visit manually. We never see them.
- Results are cached **locally in your browser** (`chrome.storage.local`) for up to 30 days,
  only so repeat lookups are fast and Purdue's servers aren't re-queried. You can clear this
  anytime by removing the extension or clearing extension storage.
- Text you highlight is read **only when you invoke Pete** (clicking the chip, the context-menu
  item, or the popup) and is used solely to extract a course code. It is never stored or
  transmitted anywhere except as part of the lookup to Purdue's public site.

## What we never do

- No collection, sale, or sharing of personal data — there is none to share.
- No reading of pages beyond the text you explicitly select.
- No third-party services, ad networks, or telemetry of any kind.

## Affiliation

Pete is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Purdue University. "Purdue" and
"Purdue Pete" are trademarks of Purdue University. All results are unofficial — confirm with
your academic advisor or the Purdue Office of the Registrar.
